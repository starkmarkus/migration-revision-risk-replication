# Source attribution and reuse

The data freeze is 18 October 2026. The two included New Zealand workbooks are retained unchanged. The other 208 original workbooks are retained separately and are not included in this compact package. Frozen extracted vintage panels reproduce the statistical analyses without downloading those originals. The source manifests identify each file, its provider URL and its SHA-256 checksum. Derived panels and statistical summaries are the study's transformations of those published aggregates, not new official estimates. Neither agency endorses this study.
